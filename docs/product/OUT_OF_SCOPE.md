# Out of scope

Status: DRAFT. This is the authoritative exclusion list proposed for scope 1.0. Exclusion does not remove anything listed in `LAUNCH_SCOPE.md` or the canonical required inventory.

- Native iOS/Android applications.
- Student social network, peer messaging, public discussion forums or unrestricted teacher/child private messaging.
- External tutor marketplace, multi-school enterprise tenancy or full school administration.
- SCORM/xAPI interoperability and generic LMS replacement features.
- Autonomous AI tutor, generative AI assistant or AI content generation.
- Points economy, badges platform, leaderboards and advanced gamification. Course completion/certificates remain required.
- Unrelated CRM, marketing automation, child marketing and speculative third-party integrations.
- Microservices, vector databases and embedding-based repository memory.
- Subscriptions, instalments, recurring payment plans, coupons, discounts, marketplace settlement, multi-currency or shopping cart. One upfront AUD payment per child/cohort is required.
- Automatic live-class recording, recording publication and a video conferencing implementation. Approved educational video resources remain required.
- Parent global scheduling, self-service unrestricted cohort transfers, guardian linking by name/email discovery or automatic child claiming. Verified admin-assisted corrections remain required.
- Teacher financial administration, including role-combination loopholes.
- Unbounded recurrence editing, a bidirectional Google Calendar scheduling authority and arbitrary third-party video embeds.
- Child DOB, address, personal email/phone, sensitive health diagnoses, government documents or unrestricted sensitive free-text fields by default.
- Production feature implementation during bootstrap. Planning, contracts, tooling skeletons, operating instructions, skills, manifests and deterministic validators are allowed.

Future implementation agents must not infer approval from convenience, provider capability, a TODO, an attractive UI pattern or the difficulty of implementing a required behavior. New scope requires the explicit human scope-change process in `SCOPE_LOCK.md`.
