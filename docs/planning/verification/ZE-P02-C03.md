## Exact unresolved contract

API-STUDENT-SELF and API-STUDENT-PREFERRED, and their StudentProfileService methods,
explicitly require an active/completed eligible enrolment and released pinned
curriculum. Their request schemas contain no enrolment/cohort/revision/resource
selector. StudentSelfProfileView contains only own registration/presentation data.
DATA_ACCESS_BOUNDARIES.md's Student self projection requires authenticated linkage
to exactly the own student. STU-019 requires safe own profile management.
These are not proven logically contradictory: the stronger gate could also apply.
The unresolved question is the exact additional eligibility/release predicate.

A server-resolved EXISTS predicate could be compatible, but the contracts do not
say whether any enrolment qualifies or which released revision/module/lesson must
exist. C02 ResourcePolicy.education requires a concrete resource UUID. Inventing
one of these interpretations or removing the gate silently would decide a sensitive
authorization boundary rather than implement a settled contract.

## Decision needed

- Approve own active student-account/profile access independent of enrolment and
  curriculum release; preserve own-identity checks and the existing safe field list.
- Or retain the extra gates and specify the qualifying enrolment statuses, whether
  any enrolment qualifies, and the curriculum release unit/predicate for these two
  profile-only operations.

No option is approved or implemented. A changed locked contract must follow
SCOPE_CHANGE_PROCESS.md with explicit owner instruction and human-merged approval
